Passenger use only: MotionClue is intended solely for passengers and must never be used while driving or operating a vehicle.

Not a medical device. Intended to help reduce motion-sickness symptoms; results vary.

MotionClue reduces motion sickness (kinetosis) when using your phone as a passenger in a moving car, bus, train, or boat.

When reading or watching content in a moving vehicle, sensory conflict occurs: your inner ear feels acceleration, braking, and turns, while your eyes look at a static screen. MotionClue renders subtle animated markers along screen edges that move in real time with physical forces, providing a peripheral visual motion reference.

Key Features:
- Native C++ sensor fusion engine running on a dedicated thread outside the JVM
- Second-order damped spring model calibrated to vehicle acceleration
- GPS speed integration for speed-proportional flow along edge margins
- Vehicle profiles: Car, Bus/Metro, High-Speed Train, and Boat/Ferry
- Adaptive brightness: markers dim in darkness and brighten in direct sunlight
- Pocket detection: proximity sensor automatically pauses the overlay
- Walking detection: step detector pauses cues when on foot (requires ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION setup on Android 10+)
- Display rotation aware: seamless transitions across portrait and landscape
- Offline operation: declares no network permissions, telemetry, or analytics
- Open source under GPL-3.0
