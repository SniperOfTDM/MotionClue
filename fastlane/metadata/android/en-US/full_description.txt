MotionClue reduces motion sickness (kinetosis) when using your phone as a passenger in a moving car, bus, train, or boat.

When reading or watching content in a moving vehicle, sensory conflict occurs: your inner ear feels acceleration, braking, and turns, while your eyes look at a static screen. MotionClue renders subtle animated dots along the screen edges that move in real time with physical forces, giving your peripheral vision the visual motion reference your brain needs.

Key Features:
- Native C++ sensor fusion engine running on a dedicated thread with zero garbage-collection latency
- Spring-damped motion dynamics calibrated to physical vehicle acceleration
- GPS speed integration for speed-proportional flow along edge margins
- Vehicle profiles: Car, Bus/Metro, High-Speed Train, and Boat/Ferry
- Adaptive brightness: dots dim in darkness and brighten in direct sunlight
- Pocket detection: proximity sensor automatically pauses the overlay
- Walking detection: step detector pauses cues when on foot
- Display rotation aware: seamless transitions across portrait and landscape
- 100% offline: zero network permissions, zero telemetry, zero analytics
- Open source under GPL-3.0
